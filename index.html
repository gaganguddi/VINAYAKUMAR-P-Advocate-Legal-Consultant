FAQ

Common questions

How do I book a consultation?

Use the form below, call the office, or message on WhatsApp. You will receive a confirmation of the time slot.

What documents should I bring for a legal notice?

Your ID proof, relevant agreements or receipts, previous correspondence and a short written timeline of events.

How long does a case usually take?

It depends on the court, type of matter and its complexity. A realistic estimate is given after reviewing your documents.

Do you handle matters outside Bengaluru?

Matters before the High Court of Karnataka and courts across the state can be handled. Other locations can be discussed on request.

Are consultations confidential?

Yes. All communication between advocate and client is privileged and kept strictly confidential.
